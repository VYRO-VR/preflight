Wearing & Placement

Slide each tracker into its tray with the USB port facing down, then secure the strap snugly so the tracker doesn't shift while you move.

  • Hip: along the belt line, centered on the front or back of the body.
  • Thighs: low on the thigh, close to the knee, rotated slightly outward.
  • Ankles: just above the ankle on the outer side of the leg.
  • Feet: on top of the foot, closer to the inner edge (foot-rotation sets).
  • Chest: on the sternum — higher on the chest tracks better.
  • Arms: on the outer upper arm (arm-tracking sets).
