Wearing & Mounting Your IBIS Trackers

Thread the velcro strap through the mounting tray with the silicone facing away from the strap, then back through with the velcro facing up. Attach the hook and secure the strap to your body.

Slide the tracker into the tray with the USB port facing DOWN.

  • Waist: centered on the lower back, just above the hips.
  • Thighs: on the outer side, roughly halfway between hip and knee.
  • Feet: on top of the shoe / foot, laces side up.
  • Chest: centered on the sternum (Full Body set).
  • Elbows / arms: on the outer forearm (Advanced / Full Body sets).
