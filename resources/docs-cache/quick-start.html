IBIS Quick Start

  1. Install and launch SlimeVR Server.
  2. Plug the receiver into the USB extension cable and place it above your play space.
  3. Press each tracker once to power it on — it appears in SlimeVR Server.
  4. Assign trackers to body parts and mount them.
  5. Perform a full reset in an I-pose, then a mounting reset.
